# Optional: add your panel, meter or battery to the wall

The card draws a small electrical panel where the power lines meet, and a
battery if you have one. If you'd rather have the real thing in the picture,
you can ask the tool to paint it onto your house. Totally optional. I
like the drawn one fine.

Do this after the day picture is final, but before you trust the night
picture. You'll need to redo night with the same change.

## Edit the day picture

Attach your finished day picture to the same chat.

```text
Edit this exact image. Add ONE realistic [outdoor electrical service panel
with a smart meter / wall-mounted home battery / solar inverter] mounted flush
on the [front / side] wall of the [garage / house], [exact spot using
landmarks, e.g. "between the side gate and the downspout, about waist
height"]. [What it looks like: color, size compared to something nearby,
conduit running up into the eave and down into the ground].

It must sit in correct perspective on that wall: vertical edges stay vertical,
horizontal edges follow the wall's lines, foreshortened as needed, with a soft
contact shadow that matches the lighting.

Change NOTHING else: same framing, camera, resolution, house, roof, panels,
plants, sky and colors. No text, labels, logos or glow.
```

Describe where it goes using things in the picture ("left of the garage
door", "under the second window"). "On the side" alone gets you a random spot.

## Then the night picture

```text
Now edit the night image the same way: add the same [panel / battery] in the
exact same spot, at night, slightly darker, [battery status light glowing
softly]. Same camera, framing and size. Change nothing else.
```

Then check that day and night still line up (see [house-night.md](house-night.md)).

## In the card

Once the real one is in the picture, open the card editor, drag the drawn
panel (or battery) right on top of it so the lines start in the right spot,
and turn off "show" for the drawn one.

Battery support in DayMill is still beta. I don't have one, so tell me if
something looks wrong.
