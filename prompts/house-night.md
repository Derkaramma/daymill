# Step 2: the night house

Stay in the same chat, right after you've got a day picture you like.

## The prompt

```text
Make the exact same image with the exact same camera, framing, house and
composition, but at dusk/night. Deep blue sky, warm light glowing from
[some / all] windows, the [garage / porch] lights on, [path lights glowing],
and the solar panels dark. Nothing else changes: same position and size of
everything, same image dimensions.
```

Tweak the lights to match your house. If you don't have path lights, take that
part out or it'll happily add some.

## Check that it lines up

This matters more than how pretty it is. The card draws the power lines in
the same spot on both pictures, so the house can't move.

Easy ways to check:

- Flip back and forth between the two pictures in your photo viewer. The roof
  edges, the garage and the driveway should stay perfectly still.
- Or open both as layers in any photo editor, set the top one to 50%, and look
  for double edges.
- Both files should be the same width and height.

If the house shifted, zoomed or the camera moved, see "Night version doesn't
line up" in [fix-it.md](fix-it.md).

Don't want to bother? Skip the night picture. The card darkens the day
picture after sunset and it looks fine.

Save it as `house-night.jpg`.
