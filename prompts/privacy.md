# Privacy: crop first

Before you upload photos of your house anywhere (ChatGPT, Gemini, a forum, a
GitHub issue), take two minutes and crop or blur:

- **House number.** On the curb, by the door, on the mailbox, on the garage.
- **Street signs** and anything with your street name.
- **License plates** on your cars and your neighbors' cars.
- **Neighbors' houses and people**, if you can. They didn't sign up for this.
- **Anything with your name on it**: packages on the porch, a gate sign.

The roof view from Google Maps or Earth is fine to use, but crop it down to
just your roof. Don't include the street names or the map pin.

Phone photos can also have your GPS location hidden inside the file. Taking a
screenshot of the photo, or exporting it with location turned off, gets rid
of it.

- iPhone: in the share sheet, tap **Options** and turn off **Location**.
- Android: most gallery apps have a "remove location" option when sharing.
- Mac: in Preview, **Tools > Show Inspector**, then the GPS tab, **Remove Location Info**.

## What DayMill does with your pictures

Nothing leaves your house. The pictures you add in the card editor get
uploaded to your own Home Assistant and stay there. DayMill doesn't send
anything anywhere, and the website doesn't store anything.

One thing to know: a picture uploaded to Home Assistant (or put in
`/config/www`) can be opened by anyone who has its link. The link is long and
random, so nobody's going to guess it, but don't post it publicly. And it's
one more reason to crop the house number out first.

The finished render is still a picture of your house. Think twice before
posting a screenshot of your dashboard with it in a public forum. When I share
screenshots I use the demo house instead.
